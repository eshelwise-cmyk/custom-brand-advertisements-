# Fictional Example: Northline Coffee

This example is fictional and exists only to demonstrate the workflow.

## Brand context

- Audience: remote professionals who want a reliable morning ritual.
- Pain: rushed mornings and inconsistent coffee quality.
- Desire: a calm, deliberate start to the workday.
- Offer: premium home coffee subscription.
- Campaign goal: communicate the feeling of a controlled morning rather than simply showing coffee.

## Scene concepts

### Pain
A remote worker opens a laptop before sunrise while the kitchen is cluttered and the morning feels compressed. The coffee ritual is the one organized element in the frame. Reserve clean space on the left for copy.

### Desire
A quiet morning kitchen with warm daylight, a prepared coffee setup, an open notebook, and an uncluttered workspace. The subject is secondary to the sense of calm control. Reserve space above the counter for a headline.

### Transformation
The same workspace later in the morning, now orderly and focused. The coffee setup anchors the composition while the rest of the environment communicates readiness.
