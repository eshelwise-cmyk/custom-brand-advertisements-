# Advertising Quality Control Skill

## Goal

Catch weak generations before they enter the reusable brand asset bank.

## Review gates

### Brand consistency
- matches the locked visual direction;
- uses approved colors and visual motifs where applicable;
- does not introduce a conflicting style.

### Audience relevance
- communicates a known pain, desire, objection, aspiration, or benefit;
- the scene would make sense to the intended audience;
- the emotional signal is clear without requiring an invented story.

### Advertising usability
- focal point is clear;
- copy-safe area is usable;
- product or brand presence is correct;
- aspect ratio fits the destination;
- no distracting artifacts or accidental visual claims.

### Anti-slop test
Reject if the result could be swapped into a random company's feed without changing its meaning. Reject generic stock-photo behavior, arbitrary decoration, and style drift.

## Scoring

Score 0-2 for each gate:

- brand consistency;
- audience relevance;
- distinctiveness;
- emotional clarity;
- composition;
- production usability.

Default approval threshold: 9/12, with no zero in brand consistency or advertising usability. Campaigns can set a stricter threshold.

## Failure handling

Record the failure category and adjust the smallest relevant variable. Do not rewrite the entire prompt after every failure. Preserve useful information from approved generations.
