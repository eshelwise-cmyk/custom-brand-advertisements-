# Ad Concept Generation Skill

## Goal

Turn audience psychology and a locked visual style into advertising scenes that communicate an emotion or outcome rather than generic product imagery.

## Inputs

- brand context;
- locked style;
- campaign objective;
- offer;
- audience segment;
- channel;
- aspect ratio;
- required product or brand elements.

## Scene recipe

Each concept should specify:

1. audience segment;
2. psychological trigger: pain, desire, objection, aspiration, proof, or transformation;
3. protagonist or subject;
4. environment;
5. action;
6. emotional state;
7. visual metaphor when useful;
8. focal point;
9. composition and negative-space location;
10. product/brand presence;
11. copy-safe area;
12. provider-neutral generation prompt;
13. provider parameters inherited from the style lock.

## Anti-slop rules

Avoid:

- generic smiling people holding products;
- irrelevant luxury imagery;
- random office scenes;
- visual clichés that do not connect to the audience;
- decorative elements without a communication role;
- conflicting art directions;
- text rendered inside generated images unless explicitly required and supported;
- invented product capabilities or claims.

## Output

Produce concepts in a structured list. Explain the intended audience signal in one sentence so a human can reject weak concepts before generation.
